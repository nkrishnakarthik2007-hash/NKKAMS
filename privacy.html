Your attendance data deserves respect.

1. Scope and roles

NKKAMS is an organisation-based attendance management project. Organisations create workspaces and manage their own members and attendance. The organisation that decides why member information is collected should tell its members about that use and establish an appropriate lawful basis. The precise legal roles of an organisation and the NKKAMS operator depend on the service arrangement and must be confirmed before commercial deployment.

For India, review the Digital Personal Data Protection Act commencement notification and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. Their provisions have a phased commencement schedule. This page is an operational privacy notice, not legal advice or a legal compliance opinion.

Important: NKKAMS is an evolving project. This notice describes the current intended product behaviour; it is not a legal certification, security audit, or promise that every statutory requirement has been independently verified.

2. Information the service may handle

  • Organisation setup: organisation name, type, location and business contact details.
  • Account details: name, Employee ID, role, organisation membership and optional email or phone number.
  • Member records: student or employee name, Employee ID, batch, joining date, leaving date and active status.
  • Attendance: attendance date, Present/Absent status and the account that marked or changed the record.
  • Support and administration: password-reset requests, organisation setup requests and relevant administrative activity.
  • Technical information: authentication/session information, failed-login counters and temporary lockout status, limited browser-side preferences or unsaved attendance drafts stored on the device, and a keyed hash used for abuse-prevention rate limits (the raw IP address is not stored in the rate-limit table).

Do not enter medical details, government identity numbers, financial information or other sensitive information unless the organisation has a clear need and has separately assessed the risks and legal basis.

3. Why information is used

  • Create and secure organisation workspaces and user accounts.
  • Record, update and display attendance to authorised users.
  • Organise members into batches and prepare attendance reports.
  • Provide account recovery, support and administration.
  • Protect the service, prevent misuse and investigate security issues.

NKKAMS should collect only information needed for these purposes. Organisation owners should avoid uploading unnecessary personal details.

4. Who can access information?

Access is controlled by account roles and organisation membership. Current database policies enforce organisation-level boundaries for member profiles, students, attendance, batches, holidays and related requests.

Organisation Head

Can manage and view records within their own active organisation.

Organisation Admin

Can manage operational records within their own active organisation, subject to role permissions.

Batch Admin / Teacher

Can access member and attendance information only in the single batch assigned to their account.

Platform Super Admin

Manages the platform and organisation setup. Normal authenticated database access is restricted from cross-organisation student and attendance tables; platform administration can still involve organisation metadata and account-management information.

Batch boundary: each Teacher / Batch Admin account is assigned one batch. Database policies restrict its student and attendance access to that assigned batch. Organisation Heads and Organisation Admins retain organisation-wide operational access.

The public demo uses fictional sample data. It is separate from live organisation workspaces.

5. Security safeguards

NKKAMS uses Supabase authentication, server-side privileged functions and database Row Level Security (RLS). The service-role credential is intended to remain on the server and must never be placed in browser code. Public pages do not need access to private attendance records.

  • Use a unique password of at least 12 characters and never share it.
  • Sign out on shared devices and keep your device/browser updated.
  • Organisation owners should remove access promptly when a user leaves.
  • Report suspected account misuse or incorrect access promptly.

Security controls are being improved continuously. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Independent penetration testing, backup/restore testing, incident-response exercises and a full security assessment are still required before high-risk or large-scale production use.

6. Storage and retention

Workspace records remain in the service while needed for the organisation's attendance and administrative purposes. NKKAMS does not yet provide a complete self-service retention schedule or automated deletion workflow. Organisation owners should define retention periods and request secure deletion when records are no longer required. Backups and provider retention periods may affect when deletion is fully completed.

7. Your choices and requests

If your information is inaccurate, you need access to your records, or you want to raise a deletion or privacy request, contact your organisation's Organisation Head or administrator first. They manage the records in their workspace. For a platform-level concern, use the NKKAMS website's public contact/request channel and clearly mark it as a privacy request.

Organisation owners should provide their members with a clear notice, explain the attendance purpose, keep information accurate, handle requests promptly and avoid retaining records longer than necessary.

8. Children and student records

Schools and other organisations may add information about children. An organisation must assess applicable parental or guardian notice/consent requirements and other legal obligations before entering children's personal data. NKKAMS has not been independently assessed or certified for child-data compliance. Do not upload children's data for real operational use until the organisation has completed that assessment.

9. Hosting and service providers

The public website is published through GitHub Pages. Authentication and the application database are provided through Supabase. These providers process technical data as needed to deliver their services and are subject to their own terms and privacy documentation. Review their current documentation before production onboarding.

10. Contact and updates

For an account or attendance issue, contact your organisation administrator. For a platform privacy question, use the NKKAMS public website contact channel and include “Privacy request” in your message. Do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive data.

This notice may change as the product, security controls and applicable legal requirements evolve. The current version will be published on this page.
